# Valley Life — Features

Valley Life is an open-ended farm life simulation game inspired by Stardew Valley, built with three.js so it runs in a web browser.

## Core features

1. **Farm management** — Inherit Oakridge Farm outside Willowbrook and restore it by clearing land, tilling soil, planting seasonal crops, and watering them daily.
2. **Crop system** — Plant seasonal seeds that grow over several days and must be harvested before the season changes.
3. **Animal husbandry** — Build barns and coops to raise animals that produce goods such as eggs and milk. Supports multiple animal types: chickens (various colors), cows, goats, sheep, pigs, rabbits, ducks, dinosaurs, ostriches, turtles, cats, dogs, and horses.
4. **Mining** — Explore a nearby cave system, break rocks with a pickaxe to collect ores, and fight monsters with a sword.
5. **Foraging** — Collect wild items found around the valley.
6. **Fishing** — Catch fish in rivers, lakes, and the ocean.
7. **Combat** — Switch between tools and weapons while exploring deeper mine levels where more valuable treasures and stronger monsters appear.
8. **Skills** — Level up farming, foraging, fishing, mining, and combat by performing related actions.
9. **Energy system** — Each morning begins with a full energy bar that decreases as tasks are performed; replenish by eating food.
10. **Time system** — Time is divided into day-length segments across four 28-day seasons; each morning starts with full energy.
11. **Relationships** — Interact with townspeople and give gifts to build relationships over time.
12. **Marriage and family** — Marry one of the eligible townspeople of a different gender; relationship points must reach a threshold. The spouse can help with daily farm chores, and the couple may choose to have children.
13. **Community Center bundles** — Complete bundles by collecting a set amount of goods and delivering them to the Community Center to unlock new areas and activities.
14. **Farm types** — Choose from several farm types, each with a unique theme and different benefits/drawbacks that help focus on upgrading different skills faster.
15. **Multiplayer** — Play online with other players (added as a post-release feature).
16. **Economy & shops** — Buy seasonal seeds and supplies with Gold, and ship produce for nightly Gold earnings.
17. **Quests, events & achievements** — Complete story and bulletin quests, experience seasonal festivals and heart events, and unlock achievements.
18. **Cooking & crafting** — Cook stamina-restoring meals and craft farm machines from gathered materials.

## Game world

- Top-down perspective rendered with three.js.
- Open-ended, nonlinear gameplay with no fixed win condition.
- Four seasons, each 28 days long.
- Willowbrook as the central hub with townspeople, shops, and Willowbrook Hall.

## Technology

- three.js for 3D rendering in the browser.
- Vanilla JavaScript game logic.
- Runs in a web browser without native dependencies.
- **Economy, Quests, Events, Cooking, Upgrades & Shipping** — Buy seeds/animals/supplies, complete quests, view storyline events, unlock achievements, cook & craft, upgrade tools at Garrick's Smithy, and ship produce overnight via the Shipping Bin ledger.
- **Procedural 3D Assets & Animations** — Articulated characters with held tools, 16×4 crop growth stages, seasonal foliage, farm/town buildings, all 13 animals, and mine monsters built procedurally in Three.js.
